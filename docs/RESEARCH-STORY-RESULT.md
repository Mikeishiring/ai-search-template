The account retains the existing-form design tradeoff, the owner/approver mistake and nested correction, another contributor's diagnosis of a date fault, reported later use, and a personal reflection about making handoffs clearer. It preserves the supervised trial's seven-of-nine observation, missing raw rows and lack of measured time savings. An unbuilt reminder remains an offer. The excluded source and future record were not read.

Manual review rated ten rubric themes fully represented and one partial: Contributor 02 retains policy and approval credit, but the narrative does not explicitly credit their original proposal. The first account remains a draft. Reading every available record did not eliminate semantic omission.

## Repairs and additional checks

The first draft check rejected incomplete evidence for an attribution. The researcher repaired its citation and resubmitted within the same run; no implementation change or human hint was supplied.

A Windows UTF-8 transport defect corrupted a dash in the sheet's work-period label. The original model tool arguments contained the correct text. The first output was preserved, the transport was fixed, and the same captured calls were replayed without a new model call or story edit. The final compiler also removes duplicate copies of qualifications already placed beside their claims. These are disclosed post-run repairs, not a second successful model trial.

An offline [policy replay](../ai-search-mvp/runs/sheet/local-policy-replay.json) then passed the same calls through setup, selected-export import, the local research server and sheet building. Six selected locations admitted 21 records and excluded two. No private user data or new inference was used. Separate regression tests exercise DM/private-channel permissions, exact files, dates, metadata-only retention, disabled model processing and policy revocation during active or cached reads.

The initial 116 tests and source boundaries were checked by an independent verifier. The later policy checks were completed by the implementation assistant and were not independently reviewed. Visual rendering remains unverified under the existing local-file browser restriction; embedded data, generated HTML and source preservation were checked directly.
